---
title: Source, Code, Print
---

SOURCE CODE PRINT is an ongoing research and design initiative that examines publishing as an expanded, hybrid practice across digital systems, code, and physical matter. Rather than treating print and digital media as separate or oppositional, the project understands publishing as an ecosystem shaped by tools, infrastructures, labor, and politics.

This database gathers writings/tools/precedents which embody this hybrid practice and organizes them through two indexical systems.The first considers the possibilities for action discussed/affected/created by these projects or as we decided to call them*, affordances: Materiality, Traceability, Ownability, Searchability, Interactivity and Updatability. The second considers the form(s) these projects are engaging/discussing, or their medium: Book, Website, Performance, Object or Data. This database is made for you to gather references so you can filter*, bookmark* and export* as you please.
